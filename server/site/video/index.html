Superpixel in 40 seconds

Open a competitor's site, click the Superpixel icon, and see every marketing tag with its account ID, plus the ads pointing to that domain in six public ad libraries.

What you just saw

  • Every marketing, ads and analytics tag on the site, with the account ID behind it.
  • Ads from Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft (Bing) and Snapchat ad libraries, streamed into the panel as each library answers.
  • A label on every ad (confirmed, advertiser, keyword, name), so you know which ads really point to the domain.
  • The whole scan exported as JSON. Free, MIT licensed, and it runs in your own browser.
